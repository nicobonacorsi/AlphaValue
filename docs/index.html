What CAC asks

A different question from significance.

A significance test asks whether observed performance clears a statistical bar. A track-record calculation asks how long a stable signal needs. CAC asks whether a decaying signal has enough lifetime information to clear the bar at all.

Observed significance

Did the observed signal clear the bar?

Ask whether the performance already observed is distinguishable from luck, possibly after accounting for selection.

Track-record length

How long would a stable signal need?

Ask how much observation time a signal of constant strength needs to reach the chosen evidence standard.

Certified Alpha Capacity

Is certification possible before decay wins?

Ask whether the entire remaining economic life of a decaying signal contains enough information to reach the evidence requirement at all.

Canonical scope. The interactive threshold uses the paper's Gaussian, known-volatility, exponential-decay, fixed-sample contract. Reaching the frontier means certification is feasible under that contract; it is not a profitability claim.

Theory and illustration

Mathematical guarantees, numerical constructions and descriptive evidence answer different questions.

01
Learning before decay

A reliability frontier in a specified statistical experiment.

The canonical Gaussian model puts statistical learning and opportunity decay on a common information clock. Its feasibility frontier determines whether the declared reliability contract can be met.

The guarantees require the likelihood and observation assumptions in the paper. Cash value also depends on the payoff map and its uniform regularity conditions.

02
Funding persistence

A descriptive empirical illustration.

The funding panel examines how a lifetime score relates to persistence in future signed funding, including information beyond contemporaneous funding magnitude.

This association does not establish executable hedged-trade returns. No confirmatory trading-profit validation is claimed.

03
Learning near the frontier

Residual information and cash payoffs have distinct asymptotic scales.
